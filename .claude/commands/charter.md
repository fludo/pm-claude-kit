Draft a **project charter / Project Initiation Document (PID)**.
PMI Process Group: **Initiating** · Knowledge Area: **Integration**.

Input / brief: $ARGUMENTS

First confirm the project context block (see CLAUDE.md). If a `project-context.md` exists,
read it. For anything unknown, ask once, then proceed with labelled assumptions.

Produce these sections:
1. **Purpose & business case** — why this project, the problem/opportunity.
2. **Objectives** — SMART, each with a success measure.
3. **Scope** — in scope / out of scope (be explicit about exclusions).
4. **Key deliverables & milestones** — with target dates (`[TBC]` if unknown).
5. **Assumptions & constraints** — budget, time, resource, regulatory.
6. **High-level risks** — top 5, with initial response ideas.
7. **Stakeholders & governance** — sponsor, PM authority, steering/sign-off structure.
8. **Budget & resource outline** — only from data provided.
9. **Approval** — sign-off block.

End with an "Open questions before approval" list. Do not invent figures or dates.
Offer to save the result to `docs/charter.md`.
