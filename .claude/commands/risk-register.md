Build or update a **risk register**.
PMI Process Group: **Planning / Monitoring & Controlling** · Knowledge Area: **Risk**.

Input: $ARGUMENTS

Use the **risk-manager** agent's method. Produce a table:

| ID | Risk (cause → event → effect) | Category | Prob | Impact | Severity | Response strategy | Response action | Owner | Early-warning indicator | Status |

Then:
- **Candidate risks to confirm** — risks commonly missed for this sector/technology.
- **Top 5 by severity** — called out for the PM's attention.
- **Open questions for the PM** — where owners or data are missing.

Do not present estimated probabilities as if measured. Offer to save to
`docs/risk-register.md`.
