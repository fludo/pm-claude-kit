Run an **iteration retrospective** (retro) — fast and action-oriented.
Agile ceremony · PMI Process Group: **Monitoring & Controlling** · Knowledge Area: **Integration**.
(For a project/phase-level review by knowledge area, use `/lessons-learned` instead.)

Input: $ARGUMENTS

If no notes are pasted, pose a few facilitation prompts to draw out observations rather
than inventing them. Produce:

1. **Context** — iteration, period, participants.
2. **What went well** — keep doing.
3. **What didn't** — root cause, not just the symptom.
4. **Action items** — table: action | owner | by when. Every action has an owner, or is
   flagged `[owner TBC]`. Keep it to the few that will actually get done.
5. **Carry-over** — any action from the last retro that is still open.

Be faithful to the notes; do not invent what the team said or decided. Offer to save to
`docs/sprints/sprint-NN-retro.md`.
