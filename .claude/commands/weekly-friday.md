**Friday routine — end-of-week hygiene scan.**

This week's notes / updates: $ARGUMENTS

Scan the material and surface loose ends. Produce three lists:
1. **Actions without owners or dates** — every action item that lacks an owner or a due
   date, so the PM can assign them.
2. **Undocumented decisions** — decisions mentioned in conversation that aren't recorded in
   minutes or the project log.
3. **Risks raised but not in the register** — risks or concerns surfaced in discussion that
   are missing from `docs/risk-register.md` (read it if present and compare).

For each item, suggest the fix (assign owner, log decision, add to register). Be faithful
to the notes; do not invent actions, decisions, or risks. End with a short "Add to risk
register?" block the user can approve to update the register.
